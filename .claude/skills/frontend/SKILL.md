## Coding Conventions

Never use plain javascript. Always use React or Typescript.

Put distinct components into their own tsx files, unless the component is too small to justify a separate file.

## Visual Styling

Do not change the app's color scheme or the visual style of existing components without explicit human approval.

When building new components, you may choose your own styles if the component's style is unspecified.
